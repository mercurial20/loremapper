# Loremapper v1.2.2-alpha.1

Trace maps from other tools, and two worlds without water.

**Try it online:** <https://mercurial20.github.io/loremapper/>. Nothing to install, and your maps stay in your browser.

> An **alpha**: please keep a backup (`Export → Editable project`) of maps you care
> about. Maps from earlier versions open unchanged.

## Trace another map

Moving over from another map maker? **Layers → Add reference image…** lays a picture
(PNG, JPEG or WebP) over your map, so you can redraw its coasts with the brushes
instead of starting from scratch.

- **Fade it** with an opacity slider.
- **Place it:** *Move & resize* lets you drag the picture and drag a corner to resize it (hold Shift to free the proportions). *Fit* and *Stretch to map* do the common cases in one click.
- **Lock it** so it can't move by accident, or hide it. Replace and remove are there too, and every change can be undone.
- It sits on the terrain and under borders, rivers and symbols, and **never changes your terrain**.
- **It's saved with the map**, inside `.loremap` files too, and stays out of your icon library.
- **Exports:** *Include reference image* decides whether exported pictures show it. It's on by default, and the same switch is in *Export → Map image*. *Export → Editable project* can leave the picture out of one file to keep it small.

## Sand planet and Snow planet

Two new presets in **Generate**, for worlds without water:

- the old ocean basins become endless dune seas or snowfields;
- mountain ranges and plateaus keep their relief;
- bare rock shows on slopes, and on the snow planet in windswept patches;
- there are no rivers.

Every time you pick one you get a new world. All other presets build exactly the same
worlds as before.

## A few more anonymous counts

On the official site, a few more actions are counted:

- a project imported, a reference image added, *Share on r/LoreMapper* clicked;
- which tools, map styles and projections were used, once per visit for each;
- when generating, exporting or importing fails.

Existing events also say which kind of map, world or export it was. Every value comes
from a fixed list and is checked before sending. Nothing you type, no map content, no
sizes, seeds or error messages are sent, and built-in worlds are counted without their
names. The README lists every event.
