# Loremapper v1.2.3-alpha.2

A small fix: no more noisy bumps when you zoom in.

**Try it online:** <https://mercurial20.github.io/loremapper/>. Nothing to install, and your maps stay in your browser.

> An **alpha**: please keep a backup (`Export → Editable project`) of maps you care about.

## Fixed

- **Close zoom shows the land as it is.** The shading used to add a fine relief texture of its own: worm-like bumps that were not in the heights, strongest on high ground even where it was flat. It is off now. Real fine detail will come from the heights themselves in a later version.
- **Gentler generated regional maps.** On newly generated regional maps the fine relief now scales with the size of a cell, so plains no longer look like badlands. Existing maps are unchanged.

## Coming next

We are rebuilding how mountains, rivers and lakes are made:

- mountain ranges you draw as lines and branch, carved by rivers into ridges and valleys;
- lakes with their own water level and depth;
- rivers that join into one network.

Each will arrive as an *alpha* option first, next to the tools you know.

## Tested

- 90 unit tests, lint, type-check and the production build pass.
- In the browser: a generated world at close zoom shows smooth landforms without the bumpy texture.
