# Loremapper v1.2.3-alpha.3

Mountains you draw as lines, lakes with their own level, and rivers that flow into the sea, lakes and each other as one water.

**Try it online:** <https://mercurial20.github.io/loremapper/>. Nothing to install, and your maps stay in your browser.

> An **alpha**: please keep a backup (`Export → Editable project`) of maps you care about. Tools marked *alpha* in the app are still being tested.

## New

- **Mountain ranges as lines** (alpha, the default in *Mountain ranges*). Draw a crest and branch it from another; rivers carve the range into ridges and valleys, and touching ranges are carved together. Set height, width, irregularity and asymmetry, try *Another variant*, or *Bake into terrain*. The old brush is still there.
- **Raise → Erosion** (alpha): paint where the land rises, and rivers carve it into mountains.
- **Lakes** (<kbd>K</kbd>): click a hollow to fill it up to its rim, or paint a lake where you want it. Shores look like the sea's, deeper water is darker, and a selected lake's level can be lowered or raised up to where it would spill.
- **Rivers as one water with the sea and lakes.** Mouths open into the water with no line across them, tributaries join without a seam, and rivers widen below each tributary. A river's end snaps to the shore or to another river while you draw.
- **Select anything** (<kbd>V</kbd>): objects, rivers, roads, realms, labels, lakes, islands, continents, seas. Click again or press Esc to let go. Islands inside lakes count as islands.

## Changed

- *Info* is now part of *Select* (<kbd>I</kbd> still works).
- Rivers take the colour of the map's water; a river has no colour of its own any more.
- The water brush is replaced by the Lake tool's *Paint*. Water painted earlier stays.
- Classic brush presets have no ragged edges.

## Known limits

- Rivers drawn before this version have no mouth or tributary links; redraw them to get them.
- Moving a river's end point does not snap it again yet; drawing does.
- Generated worlds do not use the new ranges, lakes or river network yet.

## Tested

- 108 unit tests, lint, type-check and the production build pass.
- In the browser: lakes filled and painted with many overlapping strokes; selection of lakes, islands, continents and rivers; rivers into the sea on a slanting coast, into a long lake at a shallow angle, and tributaries, from far out to close zoom.
