# Loremapper v1.3.1-alpha.2

A small release about names on the map and placing symbols.

**Try it online:** <https://mercurial20.github.io/loremapper/>. Nothing to install, and your maps stay in your browser.

> An **alpha**: please keep a backup (`Export → Editable project`) of maps you care about.

## New

- **Names on the map in groups** (right panel → *Names on the map*): Lands, Waters, Objects, Peaks, Labels, and groups of your own. A group sets the font, size on the map, colour, capitals, italic and halo of all its names. Any name can change any of these for itself and go back to its group's. A group can be hidden, or pinned so it shows even when too small to read.
- **Objects write their names under them**, and rivers and roads along them. A name goes where its object or river goes and is gone with it. Drag a selected object's name to move it, or a river's to slide it along.
- **Names tied to what they name**: *Write name on map* for a land or a sea makes a label that follows the region's name and hides when the region is gone.
- **Resize handles on all sides** of a selected symbol: a corner keeps its proportions (Shift: free), a side stretches it, Alt works from the centre. Width and height in km in the panel.

## Changed

- Symbols are placed at a size on the map (in km), the same at any zoom.
- Names too small to read hide as you zoom out and come back as you zoom in. Where they crowd, the more important ones win, and peak markers keep clear of names. Exports show what is readable at their size.
- The badge in the top bar says *alpha*, as the version does.

## Fixed

- Peak markers on flat maps could vanish near the edge of the view when it was off the map's centre.

## For older maps

- Objects that have names now show them. To hide them all, use the eye of the *Objects* group in *Names on the map*.
- A label written by hand beside an object with the same name becomes the object's name, where it was and as it looked. A turned or curved one stays as it was, and the object's own name is not drawn over it.
- The project format moves to version 6: maps saved by this release need 1.3.1-alpha.2 or later.

## Tested

- 152 unit tests, lint, type-check and the production build pass.
- In Chrome: symbols placed at different zooms come out the same size; the eight resize handles; group styles and groups of one's own (create, assign, delete, undo); names hiding and showing with zoom; dragging the names of objects and rivers; names across a planet's seam; image exports of the whole map and in the globe projection.
