# Loremapper v1.3.1-alpha.4

A small release about names: they stay readable, and the land shows under them again.

**Try it online:** <https://mercurial20.github.io/loremapper/>. Nothing to install, and your maps stay in your browser.

> An **alpha**: please keep a backup (`Export → Editable project`) of maps you care about.

## New

- **Adaptive size for names.** An adaptive name keeps its group's size on screen. Zoomed in, it does not grow past it, so the land shows (peaks are small markers again). Zoomed out, it stays readable, and hides when what it names (an island, a lake, a river) is smaller on screen than the name.
- Where adaptive names crowd, larger lands and waters come first, then rivers, then peaks; names of objects and peak markers keep clear of them.
- On by default for lands, waters (with rivers) and peaks; off for objects and free labels, which keep the size they were given on the map. Turn it on or off for a group in *Names on the map*, or for any single name in its panel. Each group sets its smallest and largest size on screen.

## For older maps

- Their name groups get these defaults: names of lands, waters, rivers and peaks become adaptive. Free labels stay as they were.

## Tested

- 170 unit tests, lint, type-check and the production build pass.
- In Chrome, on a generated island with named lands, islands, a lake, rivers, peaks and settlements: sizes on screen when zoomed out and in, small islands and short rivers hiding and showing, peak markers keeping clear of names, image exports.
