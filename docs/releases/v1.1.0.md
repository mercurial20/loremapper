# Loremapper v1.1.0

Flat maps, ready-made worlds, terrain shaped like real geology, and eight ways to draw it.

**Try it online:** <https://mercurial20.github.io/loremapper/>. Nothing to install, and your maps stay in your browser.

> Still in **beta**: please keep a backup (`Export → Editable project`) of maps you
> care about. Your existing maps open unchanged, but **the same seed now builds a
> different world** than in beta.2.

## A better start

- **Welcome screen.** The first time you open Loremapper, choose how to begin: **Generate a world**, a **Built-in world** or an **Empty map**. The same choices are behind **Maps → New map**.
- **Flat maps.** Besides whole planets, you can make a rectangle of fixed size, such as a 500 × 500 km kingdom:
  - the same scale everywhere, with plain distances and areas;
  - every tool works on it;
  - the generator builds continents, rivers and climates on it too, with a climate zone from tropical to polar.
- **Built-in worlds**, generated fresh with real relief, rivers, climate and towns:
  - flat maps: *The Inner Sea*, *The Walled Isle*, *The Thousand Isles* and *The Spine of the World*;
  - planets drawn from science:
    - *Pangaea*, Earth 250 million years ago;
    - *Pangaea Proxima*, the supercontinent Christopher Scotese forecasts in 250 million years;
    - *The Eyeball World*, a tidally locked planet with an ocean under its unmoving sun and a frozen night side.

  Have an idea for another one? **Suggest a world** opens a short form on GitHub (original settings only).

## Terrain that follows real geology

- **Relief from uplift and erosion.** Plate tectonics decides how fast each region rises, and the heights are the balance between that uplift and rivers cutting down: the *stream-power law* of geomorphology. You get:
  - mountain ranges with branching valleys and sharp ridges;
  - plains that stay low;
  - drier land that keeps more relief than wetter land.
- **Measured against Earth.** At the same resolution as NOAA's ETOPO1 relief model:

  | | Earth | Generated worlds |
  |---|---|---|
  | Mean land elevation | ≈ 690 m | ≈ 600–650 m |
  | Median slope | 2.55 m/km | ≈ 2.0–2.3 m/km (was 1.0–1.4) |
  | River-basin shape (Hack's exponent) | 0.56 | ≈ 0.45–0.47 (was 0.39–0.45) |

- **Climate with ocean currents:**
  - cold currents leave subtropical western coasts dry, like the Atacama and the Namib;
  - warm currents keep eastern coasts humid;
  - western coasts at 30–45° get Mediterranean dry summers;
  - temperatures by latitude match Earth's averages, and snow caps start around 2,800 m in mid-latitudes, like the Alps.
- **More natural plates and coasts.** A few giant plates sit among many small ones, as on Earth, and coastlines are more irregular.
- **Gravity matters.** Map settings show how tall mountains could stand at your planet's gravity and can use it as the maximum elevation.

## Eight looks

- New styles: **Anime** (cel-shaded light, flat colours, bold outlines), **Strategy game**, **Pixel art** and **Antique engraving** (shadows drawn as hatching). They join Parchment, Fantasy atlas, Clean political and Shaded relief.
- **A simpler View menu:**
  - styles as small previews;
  - one relief slider;
  - overlays as on/off chips (contours, height colours, peaks, ripples, grid).
- Hill shading starts at 30% on new maps for a cleaner look. Existing maps keep their setting.

## Easier editing

- **Kinds of land for the Raise brush:** Plains (the default), Hills, Plateau and Mountains. Plains, hills and plateaus level off at a natural height, so first strokes already look right.
- **Info tool** (<kbd>I</kbd>): point at land or water to see its name and area; click for its card, which closes with ✕ or <kbd>Esc</kbd>.
- **Hide the right panel** with the button on the map or <kbd>\\</kbd> to give the map more room.
- **Metric or imperial everywhere** (km, km², m or mi, mi², ft) from the top bar.
- **Repeat map horizontally** can be switched off for planets.
- **GitHub and Reddit** are now visible in the top bar, next to a feedback button.
- **Share your map:** after you save a map image, Loremapper offers to post it on [r/LoreMapper](https://www.reddit.com/r/LoreMapper/). Reddit opens in a new tab, and you decide what to post. You can turn the offer off.
- Map labels in Cyrillic or Greek letters (Russian, Ukrainian, Greek…) now render in Garamond instead of a system font.

## Phones and tablets

- Maps open in a **read-only viewer**: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap land, water or a feature to see what it is.
- The map gets the whole screen; the panel with layers and lands opens from its button or when you tap.
- With nothing saved on the device, explore a built-in world or open a `.loremap` file.

## Fixes

- Painting on large flat maps no longer stalls (from about 10 s per stroke to 0.1 s).
- Selecting a large landmass no longer makes zooming lag.
- Generated rivers always run downhill to the sea and join nearby river beds.

## Compatibility

- Maps from beta.1 and beta.2, saved in the browser or as `.loremap` files, open as planets with their own radius. Terrain, objects, paths, borders, labels and view are unchanged.
- New planets default to Earth's radius (6,371 km).
