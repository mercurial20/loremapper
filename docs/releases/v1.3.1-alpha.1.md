# Loremapper v1.3.1-alpha.1

A new world generator that makes land at its true size, wind and climate on flat maps, and water that finds its own way from a source.

**Try it online:** <https://mercurial20.github.io/loremapper/>. Nothing to install, and your maps stay in your browser.

> An **alpha**: please keep a backup (`Export → Editable project`) of maps you care about. Tools marked *alpha* in the app are still being tested.

## New

- **A new world generator.** Flat maps up to 10,000 km are made at their true size: a mountain range is 100–200 km wide whatever the map's size. Valleys are cut into the land, ranges have valleys along them and foothills, rivers start in the hills, islands have a massif or volcanoes, rifts hold narrow lakes. Big maps have continents of real size with seas between them.
- **Presets for small maps** (under 3000 km): part of a continent, coast, peninsula, inland, island, island and islets, archipelago, inner sea, meteorite crater.
- **Wind and climate** on flat maps (*Advanced settings*): where the wind blows from, its strength, humidity and temperature. Wet slopes facing it, a rain shadow behind a range.
- **Generated mountains are ranges** you can select and edit. *Mountain ranges* sets how much of the land they cover; drawn ranges get *Width varies*.
- **Water from a source** (alpha, River tool → *From a source*): rivers follow the slope, meander, leave oxbow lakes, fill hollows into lakes and lose water in dry land.
- **What's new**: a short tour of the news, once after an update and from the top bar.

## Changed

- Generator defaults are in the middle, as on Earth; small maps have lower peaks by default.
- Plains no longer look mountainous in the height view of flat maps.
- Planets: fewer shut-in seas and shallow blotches, Earth-like shelves, a smooth sea floor.
- Small maps generate about twice as fast.

## Known limits

- Your maps stay as they are: to get the new terrain, generate a world again.
- Generated worlds do not place water sources; their rivers are drawn paths.
- A *Supercontinent* on a 5000 km map can still have broad shallow seas inside it.
- Running a source on a big flat map takes a second or two.

## Tested

- 143 unit tests, lint, type-check and the production build pass.
- In the browser, in the height view: every preset on flat maps of 250 km to 9000 km (2048² and 4096²) and on planets, several seeds each, measured against real terrain (share of mountains, hills and plains) and checked close up; the What's new tour in English and Russian.
